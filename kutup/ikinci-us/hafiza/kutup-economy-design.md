Kütüp economy/catalog: proposed and approved by Batur on 15 Oct 2026 ("uygulayalım, test edelim, olmadı revize ederiz"). All three phases are live as of 15 Oct 2026 (migrations 14–18; shared/catalog.js holds all numbers): weekly buildings, streak freeze, ivy, Haftanın Ustası (first crown due Monday 19 Oct) and the shared monument. Also live: sound mixer (src/Sound.jsx), phone wake-lock fallback, full-screen Odak ekranı (src/FocusScreen.jsx). Paused at Batur's request; remaining board items: n03 (daylight on the site scene), n05 (village visuals 2); mascot separately.

Batur's own rules (fixed):
- Pick what to build from the catalog *before* starting; tuğla (currency) is paid out *after* the session completes.
- Abandoned build: 50% of the material cost back at once, 30% more after the ruin is cleared, 20% lost.
- No session shorter than 25 min: he said 15 min is useless ("oturup başlayamam bile, hazırlık 15 dk sürer").

Proposal core: tuğla = materials, focus = labour; nothing earns without focusing; wage grows with session length (25→25, 50→55, 75→90); cottage always free; "serbest çalışma" (no-build mode) earns half; multi-session projects; ruin is cleared by the next real session (no separate short session); rare blueprints, weekly building, Haftanın Ustası, shared weekly monument, streak with a freeze.

**Why:** He wants a psychological simulation that keeps people motivated to study long and often.
**How to apply:** Build from these rules; ask him only about genuinely open product choices. See [[kutup-work-mode]].
