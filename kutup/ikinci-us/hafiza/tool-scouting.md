Batur said (16 Oct 2026): for any job (design, motion, animation, content), research current tools, even ones released minutes ago, tell him "aga bu repo işimize yarayabilir", try it as a small demo, use it if we like it, and drop it when the job is done ("bitince de postalarız"). He sees people on Instagram doing striking design, motion videos and animation with Claude Code and doesn't want us working "ham haliyle".

**Why:** He wants the best current tooling behind his projects instead of only hand-written work.

**How to apply:** At the start of a package, run 2–3 standard web searches (extended only for very fresh or niche things), pick at most 2 candidates, and give a one-line "what it gives us / risk / cost" for each. Before running anything: read the repo (README, scripts), prefer MIT and a track record, and treat brand-new repos as riskiest. Trial in an isolated folder or scratchpad, never near `kutup/data/` or keys; markdown-only skills can be read without installing. Downloads or installs of third-party code need his OK first. Keep only what clearly beats the hand-written way; remove the rest after the job. Record what was tried in KARARLAR.md. See [[kutup-work-mode]], [[kutup-ask-before-visuals]].
